## Error Codes Added

- DETECTION_MISMATCH: Pattern bank ≠ connection bank
- PARSER_NOT_FOUND: No parser for detected bank
- CONNECTION_BANK_INVALID: Bank identifier is null/empty
- PDF_WEAK_CONFIDENCE: LOW confidence detection
- PDF_NOT_A_STATEMENT: NONE confidence (zero matches)
